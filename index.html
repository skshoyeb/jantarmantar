Background

The Cockroach Janta Party (CJP), a youth movement, along with Left groups, student unions and civil-society groups, called a protest at Jantar Mantar, New Delhi, on Sat 10 Oct 2026. They want Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar to resign and the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of voter rolls scrapped. Critics say SIR has removed more than 130 million names (NPR/AP). Delhi Police refused permission and locked down central Delhi: BNSS Sec 163 orders, about 45 metro stations shut, trains cancelled, internet cut within 4 km, and roughly 23,000 CAPF personnel deployed. Police detained CJP founder Abhijeet Dipke and leaders Saurav Das and Ashutosh Ranka on board their flight. YouTuber Dhruv Rathee was detained at his Gurugram home. Opposition leaders, lawyers and journalists were also detained. INDIA bloc street protests have been running since about 2 Oct, and Rahul Gandhi was detained on 6 and 7 Oct.

Legal context (reference)

  • Article 22(1): anyone arrested has the right to be told the grounds and to consult a lawyer. Article 22(2): they must be produced before a magistrate within 24 hours.
  • D.K. Basu guidelines (SC, 1997): arrest memo, informing a relative, and the place of custody must be disclosed.
  • BNSS Sec 163: prohibitory orders (formerly CrPC 144). BNSS Sec 172: police may detain people who resist lawful directions.
  • Note: these are general legal standards shown for reference. Whether they were broken in any specific case has not been decided by a court.
